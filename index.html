Who is Multiszn?

MultiSzn is an independent artist. Over four weeks we posted content that pulled 4,341,313 organic views with no ad spend, moved those viewers onto an email list, and sold an album to that list before dropped on Spotify.

Then we broke his email service, and thousands of people who signed up never got our emails (meaning we lost a lot of potential sales).

I included how to prevent this from happening again in the future later in this doc. 

Be skeptical. That is the correct response to anything you read on the internet, including this. The content links are live, and you can reference Multi's socials as needed.

Definitions 

I don't want to assume you know these terms, so they are here if you need them.

Fan funnel
The system that turns a viewer into a contact you own. Content → comment a keyword → automated DM → collect email or phone number → pitch offer.
Keyword comment
Asking people to comment a specific word so software can DM them automatically. Here the word was "ME".
Trial reel
An Instagram post shown only to people who do not follow you. Used to test hooks and ideas. Also used to PRINT VIEWS lol.
Segment
A portion of your email list grouped by their behavior. So, for example, your "warm" list is people who recently engaged with an email you sent.
Tag
A label stuck on a single contact so you can find them or exclude them later.
Deliverability
Whether your email reaches the inbox or lands in spam.
Open rate
The percentage of delivered emails that got opened.
CTR
Click through rate. The percentage of people who clicked a link inside.

The Offer

We ran an offer exclusively to an email list of 5,000 people over the course of seven days.

The offer bundled three things: a t shirt, the fifteen-song album, and access to the Discord community.

Twenty-five people bought. About 2,000 session views hit the Shopify page, and because session views count returning visitors more than once, conversion from those sat around 2.5%. We do not have unique visitors tracked.

Our profit margins were above 80 percent.

What Did Not Work So Well

Problem 1

His audience doesn't have much money... 

If I were to do this again, I'd add Shop Pay or financing options for people who might need it to help get more sales. We had a lot of people tell us they couldn't afford it but wanted it

Problem 2

We did not "warm up" our domain before this 

Deliverability was the main problem. Open rates were low and a lot of people messaged us saying they never got our emails.

The cause was volume. The socials blew up and we collected and sent five times more emails than we expected. The setup was not built for that load, so the messages did not land.

Problem 3

Our offer low key sucked at first 

At first, we said we weren't gonna drop the album on streaming and the only way to hear the album was to buy it. This created a ton of demand, even after the campaign technically ended, we had a ton of traffic coming in and nowhere to send them.

So, we decided it made more sense to put the music on streaming for free so everyone who wanted it could finally hear it and then give everyone who bought the album bonuses to make up for it. 

This allowed us to launch a pre-save campaign and continue running with the hype we created already. 

How We Solved Those Problems

  • Fixed deliverability so people actually see the emails. We authenticated the domain, optimized the DNS settings, and segmented the list.
  • Ran a pay what you want offer for the people who never got the album. That collects pre-saves and gives an early listen on the same page.
  • Give the people who purchased a free bundle of new music. Make them founders of the Discord as their role. They get the founders edition of the pack.
  • Personally thank every person who purchased. All twenty five of them.
  • Run a deliverability optimization automation across the whole list to get rid of inactive contacts from the campaign.

The deliverability system

How we make sure emails go to the primary inbox.

First, we fixed the DNS settings 

We authenticated the domain and optimized the DNS settings using mxtoolbox.com.

Sent emails only when people were active 

His audience opens at 12pm ET, 2pm ET, and 11pm ET, on Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays. Most sends now go out inside those windows. 

Keep the list clean

  • Actively delete any user who bounces or unsubscribes.
  • Add the tag STATUS-INACTIVE to anyone on the cold side who does not engage.

The three segments

VIP segment. On the VIP list, an active subscriber, and engaged in the last 90 days.
Warm segment. Active subscriber, engaged in the last 90 days, not on the VIP list.
Cold segment. Active subscriber who has not engaged in the last 180 days.
