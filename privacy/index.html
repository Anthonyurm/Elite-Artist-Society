Elite Artist Society

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Elite Artist Society collects, why we collect it, who else touches it, and how you get it removed. It applies to eliteartistsociety.com, app.eliteartistsociety.com, the release pages we host for artists in our program, and every tool we operate as part of the Elite Artist Society program.

Who we are. UrmetzBeatz Coaching LLC, doing business as Elite Artist Society. 664 Brighton Park Circle, Hoschton, Georgia 30548, United States. Questions about anything on this page go to support@eliteartistsociety.com.

If you are a fan rather than a member, read section 2. Most of this policy is about artists who join our coaching program. Section 2 covers what happens if you unlocked an artist's release page.

1. What we collect from members

Information you give us

  • Name, email address and phone number when you apply, book a call or join a list.
  • Answers you submit through our application form, our quizzes, and the weekly check in inside the member app.
  • Business information you choose to enter, including your song catalog, your call to action keywords, your competitor list, and the revenue and audience numbers you report each week.
  • Anything you send us directly by email or message.

Information from Instagram, only if you connect it

Connecting your Instagram professional account is optional and is never required to be a member. If you connect it, we access the following through Meta's official Instagram API, using permission you grant and can withdraw at any time:

  • Your Instagram account ID, username and account type.
  • Your own posts and Reels, including captions, media links, timestamps and thumbnails.
  • Performance metrics on your own posts, such as views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves and skip rate.
  • The text of comments left on your own posts, which we read to count how many people are asking for your music or responding to your call to action.
  • Aggregate counts of how many of your followers are in each city, which Instagram provides as totals only and never as information about an individual follower.

What we never do with your Instagram connection. We do not read, send or access your direct messages. We do not post, comment, reply or delete anything on your account. We do not access your followers list or anyone's personal profile. We request only the permissions needed to read your own content and its performance, and nothing beyond that is technically possible with the access you grant us.

Information from tools you connect

If you connect your own email platform or payment processor, we hold the credential needed to reach it on your behalf and read only what the feature you turned on requires. You can disconnect any of them at any time from inside the member app, which deletes the credential.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard log data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.
  • Analytics and advertising identifiers through cookies and similar technologies, described in section 6.

2. If you are a fan, not a member

Artists in our program can publish a release page for their music, which we host on their behalf. If you unlocked one of those pages, this section is about you. You are not a member of our program and nothing else on this page applies to you.

What we collect

  • Your email address, which is required to unlock a page.
  • Your name and phone number, if the artist asks for them and you choose to give them.
  • Whether you played the song.
  • If you pay the artist, the amount, and the city, region and country from the details you give at checkout.

Your approximate location

Our hosting provider tells us the city, region and country your internet connection appears to be in when you open a page. This is an estimate based on your IP address and it is often wrong at the level of a city. We use it to show the artist roughly where their listeners are. We do not keep your IP address alongside it and we never treat it as a fact about where you live.

Which post brought you

If you arrived from a link in an Instagram message, that link carries the Instagram username it was sent to. We use it to tell the artist which of their posts led to you, and to recognise you if you come back. If a friend forwarded you their link, we treat you as your own person rather than as them.

Who sees it. The artist whose page you unlocked. Not other artists, and not other fans. If that artist has connected their own email platform, your address is added there as well, so they can email you about their music.

Why we have it

  • To give you the music you asked for.
  • To tell the artist who their listeners are and which of their posts reached you.
  • To process a payment, if you make one.

Payments

When you pay an artist, you are paying that artist through their own payment account. Stripe handles your card details and we never see or store them. We do not hold your money and we take no fee from what you pay.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not use it to advertise to you. We do not share one artist's fans with another artist.

How long we keep it, and how to be removed

We keep it while that artist is in our program, and for up to three years after, for payment and tax records.

To be removed, email support@eliteartistsociety.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request, from the address you gave the artist. We will confirm and complete it within 30 days, other than payment records we are required to keep. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or ask us to correct it.

3. Why we use member information

  • To deliver the coaching program you signed up for, including your dashboard, your weekly check in and your content analytics.
  • To calculate performance scores and recommendations from your own content and your own reported numbers.
  • To send you program emails, reminders and updates.
  • To review applications and schedule calls.
  • To process payments.
  • To improve the program and understand patterns across members, always in aggregate and never in a way that identifies one member to another.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your Instagram data with other members, and no member can see another member's dashboard. Coaches working for us can open a member's dashboard to help them.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and program records are kept while you are a member and for up to three years after, so we can answer questions about your participation and meet our tax and legal obligations.
  • Instagram content data is kept while your account is connected. If you disconnect, we delete your access credentials immediately and stop collecting anything new.
  • Access credentials for the accounts you connect are held in a restricted database that only our backend systems can reach. They are never shown to you or anyone else, never sent to your browser, and are deleted the moment you disconnect.
  • Audio you upload to a release page is kept while that page exists. Deleting the page deletes the file.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, and the Meta Pixel to measure the performance of our advertising. Both set cookies in your browser. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's own browser add on. Blocking them does not affect your ability to use the member app.

Artist release pages do not carry our analytics or advertising cookies.

7. Your choices and rights

Disconnecting Instagram

You can disconnect at any time, from either side:

  • Inside the member app, on your profile.
  • Or in Instagram directly, under Settings and privacy, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites, by removing Elite Artist Society.

Either method revokes our access immediately and removes our stored credentials.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of the information we hold about you, email support@eliteartistsociety.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request, from the email address on your account. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days, other than records we are legally required to keep, such as payment and tax records.

You can also access, correct or request a copy of your information using the same address. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we honor those requests regardless of where you live.

8. Security

Access credentials are held in a restricted database and are reachable only by our backend systems. They are never sent to a browser and no member session can read them. No member session, and no browser, can read another member's records. We restrict internal access to what is needed to run the program. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected members promptly if a breach ever puts their information at risk.

9. Children

Elite Artist Society is for adults. The program and this website are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Artist release pages are not directed to children either. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Material changes affecting how we use your information will also be sent to members by email.
