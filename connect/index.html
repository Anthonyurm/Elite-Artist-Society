The Method

How to do this for yourself

Pick one song you already have out or one you are about to drop then work through these three steps in order.

  1. Start with the person

    Who is the one person this song is for and what circumstance are they currently in that will allow them to relate to this song?

    • Where are they and what are they experiencing when this song hits hardest?
    • How are they feeling before they press play, and how do they want to feel after?
    • What else do they already watch and follow? Those accounts are where you go to find formats that already work for your audience.
  2. Find the MEANING

    What is this song actually about?

    • If you could be known for spreading one core message, what would it be?
    • What are some personal experiences that shaped/altered your perspective?
    • What are some achievements or characteristics you have that people can look up to and be inspired by?
  3. TURN it into content

    How can I turn this into content?

    • What other interests in your life relate to your music and your audience that showcase who you are and give them something to connect to?
    • What hobbies or passions outside of music do you actually want to show off?
    • What skills or experiences do people already come to you for advice on? Which of your stories do people always want to hear again?
    • What in your real life already looks like the moment you described in step 01?
Before You Post

Run every idea through this first

This is the same lens used on the three examples above. If an idea fails more than one of these, it is not ready. Change it before you spend a day filming it.

  • Would someone who has never heard my music stop on this?
  • Does this create curiosity in the first 3 seconds?
  • Am I naming the song, or letting them go find it?
  • Does the visual match the vibe/feeling of the song?
  • Would I have shared this with somebody? If not, why not?
