Artist Pages

Release pages where independent artists offer their music to their own fans, and sell to them directly through their own Stripe account.

What an artist page is

Artists in our program get a page for each song or project they put out. A fan lands on it, hears part of the track, gives their email address to unlock the rest, and can choose to pay the artist for it. The artist keeps that fan's contact details and can see which of their posts brought each person in.

What is sold, and who sells it

Every sale is made by the artist. They sell access to their own recorded music, and in some cases their own physical merchandise such as CDs and vinyl.

Each artist connects their own Stripe account. Fans pay that artist directly and the money settles into that artist's own balance. We do not hold funds, we do not process payments on an artist's behalf, and we take no fee or commission from any sale.

How payments work

  • The artist connects the Stripe account they already have.
  • Fans pay the artist. Funds go into the artist's own Stripe balance.
  • The artist is the merchant of record and handles their own refunds and disputes.
  • We collect no application fee and receive no share of any transaction.
  • Card details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store them.

Who this is for

Artist pages are available to artists inside the Elite Artist Society program. They are not a standalone product and you cannot sign up for them on their own.

Who runs it
