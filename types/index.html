The Three Types of Music Content

Every post on your profile has a job. Most artists only post one type of content, then wonder why views never turn into real fans.

Below is each type broken down with real examples pulled from artists using them right now. Watch the clips, study why they work, then recreate the structure in your own way. Steal like an artist, don't copy.

01 · Attract

Top of Funnel Content

To attract large broad audiences to your profile by speaking to their identity, interests, or values.

Common Examples
Performance hooks
Text story clips
Low to high quality transition clips
B roll clips
Covers
Meme/game clips (think alt account)
02 · Nurture

Middle of Funnel Content

To nurture new followers by sharing personal stories, BTS content, and community moments that build trust and relatability.

Common Examples
BTS studio session moments
Live performance clips
Fan moments
Community events
Storytelling
03 · Convert

Bottom of Funnel Content

To convert your fans into customers by offering content or experiences to them.

Common Examples
Show/tour announcement videos
Merch drops or giveaways
Direct CTA content
04

How to Mix All Three

Posting only one type of content is why most profiles stall. All top of funnel gets you views from people who never become fans. All bottom of funnel makes your profile feel like a billboard. The fix is a deliberate mix.

The 60/30/10 Split
60% Top of funnel. Keep new eyes coming in.
30% Middle of funnel. Turn those new eyes into people who care about you.
10% Bottom of funnel. Give the people who care a specific action to take.

Go look at your profile right now. If your last 10 posts are all one type, you found the reason your attention isn't converting.
